Profile Summary

Shri Suman Kumar is a foundational pillar of modern Indian theatre and a lifelong custodian of Bihar’s cultural heritage. With a multidisciplinary career spanning over six decades (since 1962), he rejected the allure of metropolitan commercial cinema to serve at the grassroots level. He is widely recognized for democratizing the stage for women, rescuing regional folk formats from obscurity, and using public broadcasting as a tool for mass social reform. As a senior broadcaster, actor, director, and institutional reformer, his work seamlessly bridges traditional grassroots art with high-caliber national representation.

Key Contributions

1. Pioneering Gender Equality in Theatre

In the 1960s, Suman Kumar revolutionized Bihar’s conservative theatre scene by casting women from respected households—including a college lecturer—in his historic production of Amrapali at Bhartiya Nritya Kala Mandir. The play broke long-standing social barriers by permanently opening mainstream stages to female performers, while Kumar’s own commanding performance as Emperor Ajatashatru earned him the lifelong title "Samrat". Six decades later, he continues to empower women in theatre through ongoing productions and his role as Chief Coordinator of the annual Aadi Shakti Natya Mahotsav.

2. Social Reform & Public Activism

Suman Kumar has consistently used theatre as a medium for social change and activism. His acclaimed plays tackled pressing issues like child trafficking in Zaruraat Issi Ki Thee and rural witch-hunting in Dayeen. During the 1970s JP Movement, he co-founded the Kalakar Sangharsh Samiti with literary icons Baba Nagarjun and Phanishwar Nath Renu, braving state censorship for his anti-corruption play Andher Nagari Chaupat Raja. Beyond traditional stage productions, he directed over 1,000 street plays for UNICEF and the Bihar AIDS Control Society, conducted cross-border actor training in Purnia, and leveraged All India Radio with popular series like Loha Singh and Aur Chudiyan Jhanjhana Uthi to combat substance abuse and highlight women freedom fighters.

3. Heroism During the 1975 and 1977 Patna Floods

During the severe 1975 Patna floods, Suman Kumar navigated through floodwaters by boat and swimming to reach the isolated Fatuha transmission centre. He stayed on-site without food for roughly 70 hours to keep the emergency broadcast active, ensuring stranded citizens received critical life-saving information. He repeated this heroic effort in 1977 by traversing flooded terrain once again to operate the Fatuha transmitter for three continuous days, an extraordinary dedication later featured in a retrospective by the Hindustan newspaper.

4. Revival of Regional Roots & Folk Idioms

He stood as a bulwark against the commercial vulgarization of regional art. He integrated Bhikhari Thakur’s traditional Naach formats (including Bidesiya and Londa Nach) into modern theatrical frameworks, proving their intellectual depth. He was also a core organizational force behind the International Maithili Drama Festival (1990–2000), ensuring linguistic continuity.

5. Institutional & Prison Theatre Rehabilitation

Suman Kumar expanded theatre's reach into Bihar's correctional facilities by conducting structured rehabilitation workshops at Muzaffarpur, Beur, and Buxar jails. Rather than hosting one-off visits, he implemented an immersive method where inmates first watched a professional performance, underwent daily training sessions, and ultimately staged their own play. Officially commissioned by the Bihar government's Department of Art, Culture, and Youth, these programs provided genuine rehabilitation through creative expression, as documented in Kala Jagran's production register for the April–May 2008 workshops at Muzaffarpur Central Jail.

6. Cultural & Religious Broadcasting

For over three decades, Suman Kumar has been the defining voice of All India Radio's live commentary for Bihar’s Chhath Mahaparv, a tradition he continues to lead today. Demonstrating immense institutional trust and cultural authority, he has also served for more than 15 consecutive years as the official commentator for Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti broadcasts from Takht Sri Patna Sahib. Beyond radio, he has written and featured in nationally telecasted documentaries highlighting Bihar’s heritage, including Bihar Ka Gauravshaali Parv Chhath and Mata Mundeshwari, while continuing to organize a unique annual historical drama festival.

7. Architect of State Identity & National Representation

Cultural Productions: Suman Kumar conceived and choreographed a massive cultural production in 1997, initially featuring 76 young performers at Gandhi Maidan before expanding to 130 artists at Moin-ul-Haq Stadium. The acclaimed show went on to achieve extraordinary longevity, being performed nearly 2,000 times across various stages, including international presentations in Mauritius.

Republic Day Jhaankis: Designed and directed state cultural tableaux at Gandhi Maidan, specifically highlighting the agrarian and cultural success of Sudha.

Cinematic Excellence: Acted in landmark national projects including Richard Attenborough’s Gandhi, Prakash Jha’s Damul, Shyam Benegal’s Discovery of India, and Gulzar’s Mirza Ghalib.

संक्षिप्त परिचय

श्री सुमन कुमार आधुनिक भारतीय रंगमंच के एक मजबूत स्तंभ और बिहार की सांस्कृतिक विरासत के आजीवन रक्षक हैं। 1962 से शुरू हुए छह दशकों से अधिक के अपने बहुआयामी करियर में, उन्होंने महानगरीय व्यावसायिक सिनेमा की चकाचौंध को नकारते हुए जमीनी स्तर पर अपनी माटी की सेवा करना चुना। रंगमंच पर महिलाओं की भागीदारी सुनिश्चित करने, क्षेत्रीय लोक विधाओं को गुमनामी से बचाने और जन-प्रसारण को सामाजिक सुधार का हथियार बनाने के लिए उन्हें व्यापक रूप से जाना जाता है।

प्रमुख संस्थागत और सामाजिक योगदान

1. रंगमंच में लैंगिक समानता की शुरुआत

1960 के दशक में सुमन कुमार ने भारतीय नृत्य कला मंदिर में अपने ऐतिहासिक नाटक *आम्रपाली* में एक महिला कॉलेज की लेक्चरर सहित सम्मानित परिवारों की महिलाओं को अभिनय का अवसर देकर बिहार के रूढ़िवादी रंगमंच में क्रांति ला दी। इस नाटक ने मुख्यधारा के मंच को महिला कलाकारों के लिए स्थायी रूप से खोलकर सामाजिक बाधाओं को तोड़ा, वहीं सम्राट अजातशत्रु के रूप में कुमार के प्रभावशाली अभिनय ने उन्हें 'सम्राट' की आजीवन उपाधि दिलाई। छह दशकों के बाद भी वे निरंतर नाटकों के मंचन और वार्षिक *आदि शक्ति नाट्य महोत्सव* के मुख्य समन्वयक के रूप में रंगमंच में महिलाओं के सशक्तिकरण का कार्य जारी रखे हुए हैं।

2. सामाजिक सुधार और सक्रियता

सुमन कुमार ने रंगमंच को हमेशा सामाजिक बदलाव और जनजागरूकता के माध्यम के रूप में इस्तेमाल किया है। उनके चर्चित नाटकों ने बाल तस्करी (ज़रूरत इसी की थी) और ग्रामीण क्षेत्रों में डायन प्रथा (डायन) जैसी गंभीर समस्याओं को उठाया। 1970 के दशक के जेपी आंदोलन के दौरान उन्होंने बाबा नागार्जुन और फणीश्वर नाथ रेणु जैसी महान साहित्यिक हस्तियों के साथ मिलकर कलाकार संघर्ष समिति का गठन किया और भ्रष्टाचार विरोधी नाटक अंधेर नगरी चौपट राजा के लिए सरकारी सेंसरशिप का सामना भी किया। मंच के अलावा उन्होंने यूनिसेफ और बिहार एड्स कंट्रोल सोसाइटी के साथ 1,000 से अधिक नुक्कड़ नाटक प्रस्तुत किए, पूर्णिया में भारत और नेपाल के कलाकारों के लिए कार्यशालाएं आयोजित कीं, और ऑल इंडिया रेडियो के लोहा सिंह जैसे प्रसिद्ध कार्यक्रमों के जरिए नशामुक्ति और महिला स्वतंत्रता सेनानियों के प्रति जागरूकता फैलाई।

3. 1975 , 1977 की विनाशकारी बाढ़ में जनसेवा

1975 में आई भीषण पटना बाढ़ के दौरान सुमन कुमार नाव से और तैरते हुए पानी को पार करके कटे हुए फतुहा ट्रांसमिशन केंद्र पहुंचे। उन्होंने बिना भोजन के लगभग 70 घंटे तक वहां रुककर आपातकालीन प्रसारण जारी रखा ताकि फंसे हुए नागरिकों तक जीवन रक्षक जानकारी पहुंच सके। 1977 में भी उन्होंने फिर से बाढ़ प्रभावित इलाकों को पार किया और तीन दिनों तक फतुहा ट्रांसमीटर को लगातार चालू रखा। उनकी इस असाधारण निष्ठा का विवरण बाद में हिंदुस्तान समाचार पत्र में प्रकाशित एक लेख में दिया गया।

4. क्षेत्रीय जड़ों और लोक विधाओं का पुनरुद्धार

वे क्षेत्रीय कलाओं के व्यावसायिक पतन और अश्लीलता के खिलाफ एक मजबूत दीवार बनकर खड़े रहे। उन्होंने भिखारी ठाकुर की पारंपरिक 'नाच' विधाओं (बिदेसिया, लौंडा नाच) को आधुनिक रंगमंच के सांचे में ढालकर उनकी बौद्धिक गहराई को साबित किया। इसके अलावा, 1990 से 2000 के बीच 'अंतरराष्ट्रीय मैथिली नाट्य समारोह' के मुख्य सूत्रधार के रूप में उन्होंने भाषाई रंगमंच को जीवित रखा।

5. कारागार रंगमंच एवं कैदी पुनर्वास

सुमन कुमार ने रंगमंच का विस्तार बिहार की जेलों तक किया और मुजफ्फरपुर, बेउर तथा बक्सर जेलों में संरचित पुनर्वास कार्यशालाएं आयोजित कीं। केवल एक बार के कार्यक्रमों के बजाय उन्होंने एक गहन तरीका अपनाया, जिसमें कैदी पहले एक पूर्ण नाटक देखते थे, फिर दैनिक प्रशिक्षण प्राप्त करते थे और अंत में स्वयं नाटक का मंचन करते थे। बिहार सरकार के कला, संस्कृति एवं युवा विभाग द्वारा आधिकारिक रूप से स्वीकृत इन कार्यक्रमों ने कैदियों को रचनात्मक अभिव्यक्ति के माध्यम से वास्तविक सुधार का अवसर दिया, जिसका उल्लेख अप्रैल-मई 2008 की मुजफ्फरपुर केंद्रीय कारागार कार्यशालाओं के तहत कला जागरण के उत्पादन रजिस्टर में मिलता है।

6. सांस्कृतिक एवं धार्मिक प्रसारण

तीन दशकों से अधिक समय से सुमन कुमार ऑल इंडिया रेडियो पर बिहार के छठ महापर्व के जीवंत आंखों देखे हाल की मुख्य आवाज रहे हैं, जिसे वे आज भी जारी रखे हुए हैं। उनकी सांस्कृतिक समझ पर गहरा विश्वास जताते हुए उन्हें तख्त श्री पटना साहिब से गुरु गोविंद सिंह जयंती के आधिकारिक प्रसारण के लिए लगातार 15 वर्षों से अधिक समय से कमेंटेटर के रूप में चुना गया है। आकाशवाणी के अलावा उन्होंने बिहार का गौरवशाली पर्व छठ और माता मुंडेश्वरी जैसे राष्ट्रीय स्तर पर प्रसारित लोकप्रिय वृत्तचित्रों के माध्यम से बिहार की विरासत को प्रस्तुत किया है, और वे प्रतिवर्ष एक अनूठे ऐतिहासिक नाट्य महोत्सव का आयोजन भी करते हैं।

7. राज्य की पहचान और राष्ट्रीय प्रतिनिधित्व

बिहार गौरव गान: सुमन कुमार ने 1997 में गांधी मैदान में 76 युवा कलाकारों के साथ एक विशाल सांस्कृतिक प्रस्तुति की परिकल्पना और कोरियोग्राफी की, जिसे बाद में मोइन-उल-हक स्टेडियम में 130 कलाकारों के साथ प्रस्तुत किया गया। इस प्रशंसित शो ने अभूतपूर्व सफलता हासिल की और इसका मॉरीशस में अंतरराष्ट्रीय मंचन सहित लगभग 2,000 बार प्रदर्शन किया गया।

गणतंत्र दिवस की झांकियां: उन्होंने गांधी मैदान में राज्य की आधिकारिक सांस्कृतिक झांकियों का निर्देशन किया, जिसमें 'सुधा' (Sudha) की झांकियों के जरिए राज्य की कृषि और सांस्कृतिक सफलता को शानदार ढंग से प्रस्तुत किया गया।

सिनेमाई उत्कृष्टता: रिचर्ड एटनबरो की गांधी, प्रकाश झा की दामुल, श्याम बेनेगल की डिस्कवरी ऑफ इंडिया, और गुलजार की मिर्ज़ा गालिब जैसी ऐतिहासिक राष्ट्रीय कृतियों में अभिनय कर अपनी बहुमुखी प्रतिभा का लोहा मनवाया।

Suman Kumar: The Artistic Journey

Suman Kumar is a distinguished theatre artist and "Theatre Trendsetter" whose contributions to regional theatre span over six decades since his debut in 1962. A versatile actor and director, he has performed in over 200 stage plays and 1,000 street plays, while directing more than 150 stage productions in Hindi, Magahi, Bhojpuri, and Maithili. His career is marked by prestigious honors, including the Sangeet Natak Akademi Amrit Award and the Bhikhari Thakur Saamaan (Senior) for his lifetime contribution to the arts.

Cultural Preservation & Media

As a founding figure of Kala Jagran, Patna, Kumar has been instrumental in preserving traditional Indian theatre, specifically championing Bhikhari Thakur’s Naach format as a vital theatrical idiom for modern performance. His work extends beyond the stage to national media, where he served as a senior announcer for All India Radio and appeared in acclaimed productions such as Gandhi, Discovery of India, and Damul. He continues to shape the next generation as a mentor and teacher of acting, voice, and speech at the Bihar Institute of Dramatics.

Advocacy & Social Change

Suman Kumar exemplifies artistic excellence through his commitment to social change, utilizing theatre for public awareness on critical issues like child trafficking through his celebrated production Zaruraat Issi Ki Thee. He has collaborated with major international and national organizations, including UNICEF and the Bihar AIDS Control Society, to lead impactful street theatre workshops and campaigns. Since 1992, he has furthered this mission as a Chief Trainer and Camp Director for children's theatre workshops, fostering social consciousness and creative empowerment in youth across the region.

The Bhikhari Thakur Legacy

Preserving Bihar's Folk Theatre Tradition: The Naach Format and Bidesia

Who was Bhikhari Thakur?

Suman Kumar is dedicated to preserving and revitalizing the legacy of Bhikhari Thakur, often referred to as the "Shakespeare of Bhojpuri" .He is a leading proponent of the Naach format, utilizing it as a vital theatrical idiom for modern Indian theatre.

The Government of India recognizes Bhikhari Thakur as a cultural icon and his work as Intangible Cultural Heritage of Bihar.

Suman Kumar's Contribution

Suman Kumar has been instrumental in keeping the Bhikhari Thakur tradition alive:

Artistic Innovation:

Directed and performed Bhikhari Thakur's masterpiece across Bihar, Jharkhand, and UP, reaching over 100,000 audience members.He has integrated Thakur’s Naach format into contemporary performances, helping to maintain its relevance in modern settings.

Youth Engagement:

Suman Kumar directs Thakur's Natch Party folk plays specifically for children, ensuring the traditional style is passed down to younger generations. He has also revived this lesser-known Bhikhari Thakur play, maintaining Bhojpuri dialect.

Lifetime Achievement:

He received the Bhikhari Thakur Sammaan (Senior) from the Government of Bihar for his lifetime contribution to theatre.

Achievements & Milestones

Co-Founded Kala Jagran

Co-founded Kala Jagran in 1990 and served as its Founding Secretary, establishing a vibrant platform for modern Hindi, Magahi, Bhojpuri, and Maithili theatre. Through Kala Jagran, he created a permanent, free workspace for artists, directed over 300 plays, and curated prominent annual national theatre festivals.

National Recognition

Conferred the prestigious Sangeet Natak Akademi Amrit Award by the Vice President of India for his lifetime contribution to Indian theatre[cite: 1]. He was also honored with the Lifetime Achievement Award (2025) and the Bhikhari Thakur Samman (Senior) by the Government of Bihar.

Landmark Production

Revitalized the traditional Bhikhari Thakur Naach format, effectively bridging folk idioms with modern Indian theatre. Directed and performed in regional productions across Bihar, Jharkhand, and Uttar Pradesh. Directed the acclaimed play Zarurat Isi Ki Thee on child trafficking, which was invited to the National School of Drama's prestigious Jashne Bachpan Festival.

International Collaboration

Suman Kumar’s work extended onto international platforms through film and cultural exchanges. He acted in Sir Richard Attenborough’s Academy Award-winning film Gandhi and served as Technical Director for the Indo-Russian Dance Festival organized by the Government of Bihar. Additionally, his landmark cultural production, Bihar Gaurav Gaan, was presented internationally in Mauritius.

Educational Initiative

Teaches acting, voice, and speech at the Bihar Institute of Dramatics. His extensive leadership record includes serving as a Chief Trainer for state-sponsored children’s workshops since 1992 and frequently judges inter-college and inter-school theatre festivals at prestigious institutions like IIT Patna, Patna Medical College, and Magadh University.

The Magic of Voice: Suman Kumar's Perspective

Suman Kumar reflects on his journey from a casual announcer in 1970 to a retired veteran, emphasizing that a deep connection to literature and the "inner voice" is vital for the craft. He advocates for the preservation of Bihar's folk theater styles, warning that these cultural treasures will fade unless we protect the village-level traditions and the artists who sustain them.

Safeguarding the Roots of Rural Culture

In this discussion on Bihar's theatrical styles, Suman Kumar argues that "folk" is the fundamental essence of the region’s identity. He stresses that cultural heritage cannot be saved in isolation; rather, the culture will only survive if we actively support village-level artists and promote the rural lifestyle that birthed these traditions.

February 15, 2015
Reviving History through Drama: Historical Theatre as a Lesson for the Future

This report covers a historical drama festival at Kalidas Rangalaya featuring the play 'Andha Yug', which was directed by Suman Kumar. Ram Kripal Yadav was also present at the event. The All India Theatre Festival Launch: Bhikari Thakur Samman Laureate: Suman Kumar

Decades of live performances, cultural milestones, and critical breakthroughs come together in this press archive. The Lifeline of Patna Theatre: Training Future Artists

A comprehensive feature marking the foundation day of Bihar Art Theatre at Kalidas Rangalaya. Suman Kumar is recognized as a master trainer whose continuous dedication at the Bihar Natyakala Prashikshanalaya expanded student intake from 30 to 60, bringing a wave of female artists into mainstream theatre and preserving its cultural soul.

Policy & Arts: Bringing Artists back to Legislative Spaces

Suman Kumar addresses a crucial policy gap, noting that before the Emergency, artists routinely held respected positions in the Legislative Council. He boldly asserts that the plight of local art forms will never improve unless seasoned cultural exponents are actively integrated into the government's policymaking machinery.

Struggling Rehearsals: Suman Kumar on Financial Realities

Reflecting on the 1970s and 80s when teams crowdfunded plays shop-by-shop, Kumar critiques modern state grants as merely survival-level. He highlights the critical shortage of spaces, urging the government to provide dedicated rehearsal setups like the Premchand Rangashala campus to foster authentic experiments.

August 28, 1975
Hindustan Samvad: Cultivating a Culture of Cleanliness

Participating in a prominent public forum ahead of Chhath Mahaparv, Suman Kumar speaks out on social responsibility. He challenges the public to internalize the profound purity and rigorous hygiene practiced during the four days of Chhath into a year-round civic habit, starting straight from individual homes.

The '75 floods didn't cause an epidemic..

Suman Kumar recounts surviving the 1975 Patna floods inside the Akashvani building as water seeped in at 9:15 PM while preparing for the program Hawa Mahal. After spending a hungry, mosquito-ridden night in a single upper-floor room with a colleague, AIR Director Radhakrishna reached them by boat the next morning. Despite severe flooding across Patna, Akashvani maintained emergency transmissions from Jethuli near Fatuha for 70 continuous hours to keep the public informed.

Civic Purity: Beyond Four Days of Festivity

Suman Kumar delivers an impactful commentary questioning public psychology: "Why do we immediately discard the cleanliness habits of Chhath once the festival concludes?" He urges communities to adopt permanent waste reduction and sanitation discipline instead of treating cleanliness as a temporary ritual.
